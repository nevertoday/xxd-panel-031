# XXD Panel 031 · 寓意几何民俗拓印

Turn each supplied photograph into one finished meaning-led graphic that joins folk catalogue, antiquarian rubbing, and modern design. Preserve the real photograph only in paired modes. First understand the source's core meaning, cultural character, or emotional relation; then keep one principal visual motif and only the few auxiliary forms that genuinely carry narrative. Derive a geometric parent—circle, square, triangle, arc, axis, or repeated ratio—from the subject itself, and bind motif, type, and whitespace to that structure through alignment, tangency, shared axes, nesting, mirroring, progression, or edge crop. Rough woodblock and dry-screenprint evidence lives mainly inside the shapes while essential contours and geometry stay crisp.

Operational rules follow the shared XXD Panel workflow contract: four combinable modes; source-adaptive ordinary canvases; exact 50/50 paired geometry; linked or independent four-device wallpapers; copy and locale preflight; fresh generation jobs; privacy-preserving raster generation; and one fresh task directory per source and mode. Style-specific sections may refine aesthetics, copy hierarchy, and acceptance criteria but never override this contract.

## Non-negotiable contract

- One source photo may be processed in one or more selected modes. Each selected ordinary mode (`top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`) produces one PNG; selected `wallpaper-pack` produces exactly four separate PNGs. Selecting all four modes therefore produces seven final files per source. Keep every mode in its own task directory and never combine modes, sources, or wallpapers into a grid, contact sheet, or overview.
- Resolve a non-empty ordered set of modes before generation: `top-bottom`, `left-right`, `design-only`, and/or `wallpaper-pack`. Accept one choice, multiple choices separated by `+`, Chinese/English commas or whitespace, natural-language names, or `全部` / `all`. Deduplicate repeated choices and execute in menu order 1→4. If none is specified, ask with the multiline multi-select menu in the workflow. Do not ask again when the set is already clear.
- When the selected set contains `wallpaper-pack`, require a second choice: `linked` or `independent`. A linked pack approves one anchor wallpaper, iPad by default, and makes every other device reference both the original photo and that same anchor. An independent pack gives every device only the original photo. Neither permits resizing or cropping one wallpaper into the others. Do not ask this follow-up when wallpaper mode is not selected.
- Paired modes split exactly 50/50. `design-only` and every wallpaper contain no visible source photograph, no seam, and no reserved photographic panel.
- Exact user pixels have highest priority, followed by an explicit ratio or destination. Otherwise ordinary modes adapt to the complete source: `top-bottom` = `W×2H`, `left-right` = `2W×H`, and `design-only` = `W×H`. Never impose the archived initial brief's 3:4 canvas as a silent default.
- Visible photography remains faithful. Allow only restrained editorial grading and environmental extension needed for an explicitly overridden canvas. Never stretch, distort, repaint, replace, or structurally alter the subject.
- The transformed frame must preserve identity, action, function, emotion, and supported cultural meaning through at least three source-specific cues while reducing the source to one principal symbolic motif plus very few narratively necessary supporting forms. Every visible form must have source, meaning, and structural duty within one source-derived geometric parent. It is not literal element-by-element translation, generic folklore ornament, random scatter, material collage, realistic illustration, fixed traditional palette, decorative symbol dumping, or 3D.
- Copy has no silent default. Resolve automatic copy, exact custom copy, or text-free output before image generation. Automatic and custom modes also require a target language or locale. By default, one source-specific copy package is shared verbatim across all selected modes; explicit per-mode copy instructions override it.
- Render no logo, watermark, signature, colour swatch, UI, device mockup, decorative pseudo-text, or unrelated explanatory prose.

## Aesthetic motive lock

Modes and device ratios may change placement and aspect ratio but never the 031 motive. Every transformed frame must visibly express this chain:

**this exact photographed subject or inseparable relation → preserve identity, proportion, gesture, action, function, emotion, cultural character, and at least three defining cues → determine the one supported meaning that deserves to survive → reduce the source to one core visual motif and only narratively essential helpers → derive a geometric parent from the subject's own circle, square, triangle, arc, axis, or repeated proportion → construct silhouette, negative space, enlargement, merging, nesting, tangency, shared axis, mirroring, progression, or edge crop within that one system → use one dark structural ink and one or two source-derived theme inks on bright paper → keep breakage, rubbing, exposed paper, and uneven ink mainly inside crisp geometry → align restrained title and micro-notes to axis, negative shape, or boundary**.

The cultural charge comes from a source-supported meaning expressed through strict reduction, not from adding familiar “traditional” symbols. Folk catalogues, antiquarian rubbings, woodblock print, dry screenprint, and modern independent publishing are visual influences rather than a motif library.

Reject the result as generic when an unrelated photo could replace the source without materially changing the core motif, supported meaning, geometric parent, silhouette/negative-space relation, ink proportions, or type alignment. Also reject generic clouds, waves, seals, auspicious animals, borders, folklore pattern filler, random dots, material collage, literal scene translation, equal-weight layout, realistic illustration, fixed red-black-beige palettes, and decoration without semantic and structural duty.

## 031 visual system

### One meaning-led core motif

- Privately lock at least three source-specific cues across contour, pose/orientation, function, proportion, material, colour, opening, connection, overlap, negative shape, or relationship distance.
- First determine the photograph's supported core meaning, cultural property, or emotional relation. Reduce it to one principal visual motif and only a few helpers that are necessary to understand the narrative.
- People keep pose, direction, clothing mass, and relational distance; animals keep body rhythm and head direction; plants keep growth gesture; architecture keeps skyline and defining openings; objects and vehicles keep functional silhouette and negative shape; landscapes keep one source-specific terrain or spatial relation.
- Translate with silhouette, negative space, local enlargement, form merging, and symbolic reduction. Every form must have source, meaning, and structural duty; remove anything that does not deepen understanding.

### One source-derived geometric parent

- Derive a circle, square, triangle, arc, axis, or repeated proportion from the source subject's own structure as the one geometric parent.
- Bind motif, helpers, type, and whitespace to it through alignment, tangency, common axis, nesting, mirroring, proportional progression, or boundary crop. Free distribution, eccentricity, asymmetry, and crossing are allowed only when they share this system.
- Establish one clear centre of gravity, hierarchy, rhythm, and negative space: distant reading reveals order; close reading reveals print wear and cultural detail.
- Reject random scatter, decorative accumulation, unrelated geometry, average centring, and multiple competing structural systems.

### Rough ink inside precise geometry

- Mix woodblock rubbing, dry-brush screenprint, and old catalogue printing. Broken ink, abrasion, exposed paper, and uneven density belong mainly inside the shapes.
- Keep the geometric boundary, essential contour, axis, tangent, and overall layout crisp enough to remain exact. Roughness must reveal material, never dissolve structure.
- Use silhouette, overprint, cut negative space, and controlled registration variation rather than realistic modelling or decorative distress.
- Reject dirty ageing, blurred edges, vector sterility, random grunge, thick 3D relief, photographic texture collage, and distress that weakens recognition.

### Bright paper and source-derived print inks

- Use a bright light paper ground, one deep ink for the visual skeleton, and one or two theme inks derived from the current source's most recognisable and spirited colours.
- Build hierarchy through area ratios, value, and selective overprint; colour stays clean, energetic, and print-like rather than uniformly distributed.
- Preserve the source's warm/cool and emotional identity without translating every hue. Never reuse another source's ink set.
- Avoid fixed folklore palettes, muddy faux-ageing, decorative multicolour, gradients, neon, universal beige, and colour without structural role.

## Copy and typography belong to 031

031 text behaves as secondary information attached to the same geometric parent as motif and whitespace—not a conventional poster headline, folklore label, museum UI, detached caption block, or advertising overlay.

- Automatic copy derives one concise title from the source's supported theme, meaning, cultural property, emotional relation, or symbolic action without inventing heritage.
- Prefer one restrained title plus zero to two very small supporting elements. A number, place, or year appears only when supplied or reliably established.
- Preserve exact user wording verbatim. Refine an editable direction only within permission while protecting audience, mandatory terms, tone, and semantic phrase breaks.
- Apply the unrelated-image swap test: generic lifestyle words that survive on another source must be rewritten.

Typography has its own 031 construction:

- **Role:** a subordinate information layer that completes the geometric system.
- **Voice:** concise, culturally grounded, quiet, exact, and contemporary.
- **Construction:** one short title plus zero to two useful micro-notes; size and spacing preserve the core motif as the visual centre.
- **Placement:** align, nest, interweave, or crop along an axis, negative shape, tangent, or graphic boundary.
- **Material:** crisp editorial type with restrained print wear; never a decorative “ethnic” font, faux seal script, huge display headline, or archival costume.
- **Script:** use native construction, joining, direction, punctuation, spacing, and legibility for the resolved Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, or Latin locale.

Resolve locale independently from command language:

```text
target market or audience > requested output language > direction language; if none is explicit, ask before generation
```

Localise by transcreation using native wording, rhetoric, punctuation, spacing, shaping, and semantic line breaks. Never infer nationality or audience language from appearance, clothing, scenery, filenames, metadata, or signs.
